18 Anagrams

If this text persists, please check that Javascript is enabled.



When in the game, click on the letter buttons (or type) to spell a valid word. Words will range in length from 4 to 9 letters long, with three of each length. (In hard mode, they instead range from 4 to 12 letters long, with two of each length.) You have 30 seconds to correctly anagram the letters, but your time rolls over if you skip or correctly anagram a word.

The dictionary used to select words is the 5d+2a list from ESDB, taking only uncapitalised words without symbols (this includes both British and American spellings of words, but omits some common inflections on words such as plurals and -ing verbs [but it includes, for example, -ing adjectives]), but the dictionary used to validate words is a combination of that and 3of6all with the same inclusion rules. Profanities have NOT been filtered out - apologies!

A hand-coded reimplementation of 18 Words.
